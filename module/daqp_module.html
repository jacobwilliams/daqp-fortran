Object-oriented interface to the Fortran port of DAQP, a dual active-set solver for dense convex quadratic programs:

  min  0.5 x'Hx + f'x
  s.t. blower(1:ms)  <= x(1:ms) <= bupper(1:ms)
       blower(ms+1:) <= A x     <= bupper(ms+1:)

H must be symmetric positive definite, or positive semidefinite (then a proximal-point outer loop is used); with no H, the problem is an LP. Constraints can be equalities, soft, or binary (branch and bound); the constraints can be split into levels of a hierarchical (lexicographic) QP; and a nonsymmetric H defines an affine variational inequality (AVI).

Multipliers

The multipliers satisfy , with : when the upper bound of row i is active, when its lower bound is active, and 0 for an inactive row. daqp_lower_positive converts them to the convention at a lower bound (). For a hierarchical QP, lam holds the slacks of the soft levels instead.

Example

    type(daqp_type) :: qp
    call qp%setup(H, f, A, bupper, blower, istat)
    call qp%solve(x, lam, istat)

private subroutine set_soft_weights(me, istat, rho_l, rho_u, w_l, w_u)

Set individual weights of the soft constraints (one entry per constraint; zero selects rho_soft/w_soft; an absent argument leaves that weight as it is). A violation s of the lower (upper) side of a soft constraint adds w_l*s + s**2/(2*rho_l) (w_u*s + s**2/(2*rho_u)) to the objective (in the units of the problem).
